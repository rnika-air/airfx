# 2026 Trends And The 4.0 Effect Collection

The research came first. The sources are mostly trend blogs and studio posts, so they show what designers are talking about, not measured adoption.

## What the sources agree on

- Chrome, inflated and liquid type is the most repeated direction, treated as a surface, and written about as fluid and "almost alive".
- Y2K has moved from recall to fluency. Advice: one Y2K element, not five. Chrome pairs well with a clean sans-serif.
- Kinetic type is the most cited motion trend, partly because short video is often watched muted. The warning: motion must not be needed to read the words.
- Grain, Riso, halftone and analog damage are a reaction against clean AI-looking images.
- Fluid distortion and "liquid glass": translucent surfaces and smooth bending.
- Procedural, node-based and data-driven graphics. Opinions on AI split between faster production and a look designers are pushing against.
- Sources disagree on iridescence: some note that it was fairly rare in the original Y2K era and is more a feature of the revival.
