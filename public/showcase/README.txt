AirFx showcase media
====================

Put your own media here. The intro screen, the log-in screen and the About page
use these files to show real AirFx effects. Use exactly these names:

  photo.jpg       a photo (JPG, at least 1080 px on the short side, under 3 MB)
  video.mp4       a short clip (MP4/H.264, 5 to 15 seconds, 720p, no sound needed, under 10 MB)
  animation.gif   a GIF (under 8 MB; fewer frames load faster)

Only use media you own or have permission to publish: these files are public on
the website. Cloudflare Pages rejects any single file over 25 MB.
